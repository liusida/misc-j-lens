# Five prompts used for the J-Lens study

These are the exact five pretraining-style prompts used by
`five_prompt_centuries_study.py`. Prompt indices in the contribution table
correspond to the numbering below, starting from index 0.

## Prompt 1 — Astronomy

> The history of astronomy spans many cultures and centuries. Ancient
> observers recorded the motions of the Sun, Moon, planets, and stars, while
> later scholars developed mathematical models that gradually changed
> humanity's understanding of its place in the universe.

## Prompt 2 — Forests

> Forests regulate climate, protect watersheds, and provide habitats for
> countless species. A mature forest contains layers ranging from the shaded
> floor to the canopy, with fungi, insects, birds, and large mammals
> participating in interconnected cycles of nutrients and energy.

## Prompt 3 — Railways

> Railway networks transformed travel and trade during industrialization.
> Engineers constructed bridges, tunnels, stations, and long tracks that
> connected inland towns with ports. The resulting movement of people and
> goods helped cities grow and regional markets become integrated.

## Prompt 4 — Computer programs

> A computer program is a sequence of instructions expressed in a language
> that a machine can execute. Large systems are usually divided into modules
> with clear interfaces, allowing programmers to test each part, locate
> errors, and change behavior without rewriting everything.
